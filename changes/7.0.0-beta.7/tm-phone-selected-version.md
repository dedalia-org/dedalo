---
title: On a phone, the time machine marks the picked version across the whole row and brings its preview into view.
type: changed
audience: user
date: 2026-10-18
---

Two things got in the way of reading a long history on a phone.

The picked version is marked in orange, but only the value half of its Id row was filled,
so pale strips of card showed at either side of the mark and the row read as a loose chip
rather than the row you are on. The mark now runs the full width of the row, Id label
included.

And picking a version deep in the list left what you picked out of sight above you: the
preview of the chosen version and the **Apply and save** button both sat over the fold.
Picking a version now scrolls its preview to the top of the tool, just under its header,
so the version you are about to restore is the first thing you see.
