---
title: Export: columns are added and reordered by tap on a phone
type: added
audience: user
date: 2026-10-17
---

The **Export** tool built its column list by dragging: a field dragged from the
available-fields list onto the *Active elements* list to add a column, and a
selected column dragged up or down to reorder it. A finger cannot drag, so on a
phone neither could be done at all — the tool opened, but its columns could not
be chosen.

On a touch screen each field in the available-fields list now carries a **+**
that adds it as a column on a tap, and every selected column carries **up / down**
buttons and a **✕** that move or remove it. Both do exactly what the drag did (a
field already added is pointed at rather than duplicated). On a computer nothing
changes: the drag is still the gesture there.

The phone view is also straightened out around that. The fields list fills the
whole width (it shared its row with the other, empty drill column), the empty
column list names the gesture it has (it points at the **+** instead of saying
"drag a field here"), the **+**, the remove **✕** and the option checkboxes are
full 44px tap targets, the export preview fills its panel, and the result area no
longer reserves a blank half-screen before any export is run.

The **Export presets** editor (and its Search twin) opens as the full-width
sheet on a phone — maximum room for the form — while staying the small card on a
computer. And a saved preset is one tidy line in the panel on a phone — the
apply ring, the name, then Edit and Delete — instead of the desktop columns
squeezed to slivers with their labels stacked one letter per line.
