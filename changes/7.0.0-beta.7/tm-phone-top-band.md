---
title: On a phone, the time machine's two version panels, its Apply action and its page counter now stack into one readable column.
type: changed
audience: user
date: 2026-10-18
---

The time machine band at the top of the tool compared the live version and the chosen
snapshot in two half-width columns. On a phone that left the value hugging the left
edge with an empty half beside it, floated the **Apply and save** button up into the
version labels, and wrapped the page counter's chevrons onto a line of their own.

Now every block gets the full width: the live value first, the chosen snapshot under
it, then the actions. Each version is announced by a strip above its value — *Now* for
the live one, the snapshot's date and time for the chosen one. The still-empty snapshot
panel stays out of the way until a version is picked, **Apply and save** takes a row of
its own, and the page counter keeps its chevrons on one line. On a desktop nothing
changes.
