---
title: Clicking a field puts the cursor in it, every time
type: fixed
audience: user
date: 2026-10-20
---
Clicking into an Email or Number field while another field was selected
sometimes selected the field but left no cursor in it: typing went nowhere
until you clicked a second time. The same happened to Date, IRI and colour
fields, and when moving into a field with the Tab key from a related-records
list.

Now the click or the Tab always leaves the cursor where you aimed it. Clicking
the second value of a field no longer sends the cursor back to the first one, and clicking the label or the empty space of the selected
field puts the cursor back in its first box. Lists of related records, check
boxes, radio buttons and text editors keep their own behaviour, and the buttons
of a field act as before.
