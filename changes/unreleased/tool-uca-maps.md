The map of a record gains a toolbar of its own. Click any drawn object to read its
measurements and elevation, restyle it, mark its uncertainty, give it properties,
attach images, and download it as GeoJSON, Shapefile, KML or a PDF sheet. Add your
own basemaps and WMS layers to a record, upload shapefiles, GeoJSON and KML (reprojected
on the server) and georeferenced or plain images you can fit by their corners, look up
Catastro parcels and administrative units (Spain), search modern places and the
Pleiades, Pelagios and DARE gazetteers of the ancient world, keep a legend, and
download the map as a PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP or GeoTIFF that opens in a GIS. Catastro
needs an administrator to enable it for each map, and format conversions need GDAL on
the server. See the [user guide](./tools/using_uca_maps.md).
